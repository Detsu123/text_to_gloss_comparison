==================================================
1. RESEARCH QUESTION
==================================================

Primary research question:

How do recurrent sequence-to-sequence architectures and Transformer architectures compare for low-resource Text-to-Sign-Language-Gloss translation under a controlled experimental setting?

Secondary questions:

- How does vanilla RNN compare with gated recurrent architectures?
- Does GRU provide a good performance/complexity trade-off?
- Does LSTM improve long-range sequence modeling?
- Does Transformer perform better on longer sentences?
- How do models differ in parameter count and training time?
- What types of translation errors are produced by each model?

Do not assume beforehand that Transformer will win.
The conclusions must follow the measured results.

==================================================
